The data behind a proof on XYO Layer One lives outside the chain, in a Data Lake. Developers building on XYO now have a storage option for that data that cannot expire, be deleted, or be altered after the fact. XYO has added Auto Drive as a supported S3-style Data Lake in the XYO SDK.
